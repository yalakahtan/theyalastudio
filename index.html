Fair Share

An app that tells you in one screen whether you are paid fairly, then gives you a clear next step.

Role
Solo designer
Type
Self-initiated concept
Year
2026
Deliverables
2 app screens, colour & type

Problem

Pay gaps are hard to see. People rarely know what others in the same role earn.

Approach

Show one comparison only: you against the median for your role and city.

Process

Started with a grey wireframe, then used yellow for “you” so it stands out against cobalt.

Result

Three actions on the second screen: ask for a review, talk to an advisor, or share data.

Rights Atlas

The 30 articles of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights as a grid you can tap, each one in plain words.

Role
Solo designer
Type
Self-initiated concept
Year
2026
Deliverables
Article page design

Problem

The UDHR is written in legal language that many people find hard to read.

Approach

Give all 30 articles equal space in one grid, so no right looks more important than another.

Process

Tested the grid at 6 columns on desktop and 5 on phones so the numbers stay tappable.

Result

Each article page has one plain sentence, a daily-life example and a language switch.

Source: Original concept by The Yala Studio. Article 1 paraphrases the UDHR (UN, 1948, public domain).Source file: 03_RightsAtlas_Website.psd

Jiran

A neighbourhood app that matches small, local requests with people nearby who have an hour to give.

Role
Solo designer
Type
Self-initiated concept
Year
2026
Deliverables
Request feed screen

Problem

People want to help neighbours but don't know who needs what.

Approach

Make each request small and specific, with distance and time on every card.

Process

Tested card order with neighbours; distance first, then time, then the task.

Result

One green button, “I can help”, so saying yes takes a single tap.

How I work

The same four steps on every project, big or small.

1

Listen

Talk to the people the design is for, and to the team who will run it.

2

Sketch

Grey wireframes first, so we agree on structure before colour.

3

Test

Show screens to real users early. Fix what confuses them.

4

Deliver

Clean, layered Photoshop and InDesign files your team can edit.

About

Simple design for serious causes.

The Yala Studio is the graphic design practice of Yala K. Al-Douri, based in Ontario, Canada. I work across graphic design, illustration and drawing, and I have been publishing work on Behance since 2016.

My hand-drawing studies in still life, perspective, materials and technical objects train the eye I bring to digital work in Photoshop and InDesign. My rule is simple: if a person in a hurry can't understand the message in five seconds, it isn't finished.
